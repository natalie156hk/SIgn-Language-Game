This model is used for a non-commercial educational game. Changes were made to its runtime integration and preprocessing.

The model was trained using the ASL-HG dataset. Dataset credit:
Pranto et al., ASL-HG: American Sign Language Hand Gesture Image Dataset (2026)
https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/j4y5w2c8w9/1
DOI: 10.17632/j4y5w2c8w9.1

Model card reports validation results on a held-out portion of its dataset; those results do not guarantee accuracy with live school webcams.
